The vision
behind the work

The work that I intend to do is one that will serve to help brand owners - those who are truly serious about their brand - establish something that has value.

Anyone can build a brand, but only some focus on value rather than quick money, money is a byproduct, value lasts, I will help you add value to your brand.

I have a skill, perhaps a gift, a skill is something you practice, a gift is something your born with, you've probably heard the quote "Jack of all trades, master of none", but the real undiluted version finishes it with "but often times better than a master of one".

I can't sit long enough to learn one thing, but my heart is set on making clothes, the rest is just a byproduct to get to the finished piece, using my crreative mind I will act as a creative director for your personal brand.

I don't want to just sell you a quick service, you can find those people on fiverr or upwork, i'm looking to partner, drag the work as far as needed to help you get to your desireable goal.

If this is what you're looking for then trust me to help you with it, building something that has meaning and value is what truly fulfils and I want to be right in the middle of it.

I will help you conceptualize design ideas that fits your brand, and point you in the direction you should go and how you should go about it, the whole nine-yards.

Kees J R Vieira
